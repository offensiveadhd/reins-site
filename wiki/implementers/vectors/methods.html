Agent methods, their scopes, and which take a surface id

Test vectors: Agent methods, their scopes, and which take a surface id. Shown here as it is in conformance/vectors/methods.json.

Generated at build time from conformance/vectors/methods.json.

Protocol 0.2. Covers: SPEC.md section 9 table; section 6 (hub methods); section 8 (scope order).

Download methods.json: the file itself, as the conformance suite reads it.

How to use it #

Method to scope table, HUB_METHODS, READ_ONLY_METHODS, CHAT_SPEAK_METHODS, scope intersection.

Notes #

scope null means the method needs no grant scope. takesSurface follows "All but the first two and stream.close take s". hub marks HUB_METHODS, which section 6 lists.
